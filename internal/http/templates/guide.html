Watch and request media

Follow these steps to watch on your devices and request movies or shows.

Watch in a browser

Click the button below, then sign in with your {{ .MediaServerName }} username and password.

Open {{ .MediaServerName }}

Request a movie or show

Use Seerr to request something new to watch. Search for a movie or TV show, then press Request.

Open Seerr

Set up the {{ .MediaServerName }} app

  • 1. Install the {{ .MediaServerName }} app On your TV or phone, open the app store and install {{ .MediaServerName }}.
  • 2. Enter the server address When the app asks for a server, enter: {{ .MediaServerPublicURL }}
  • 3. Sign in Use your {{ .MediaServerName }} username and password.
  • 4. Use Quick Connect (easier on TV remotes) On the TV app, choose Quick Connect to get a code. On your phone or computer, sign in to {{ .MediaServerName }} and open Settings -> Quick Connect, enter the code, then approve. Your TV app will sign in automatically.
  • If it does not work Check that the server is exactly {{ .MediaServerPublicURL }} (with https), then try again. If login fails, double-check username/password.

After you make a request

  • 1. Open Seerr and request a movie or show.
  • 2. The request is processed in the background.
  • 3. This can take a little time, depending on availability.
  • 4. When ready, it will appear in {{ .MediaServerName }}.
  • 5. If it has been a long time, open an issue in Seerr for that movie or show.
